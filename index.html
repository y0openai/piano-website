Market Universe

Your strategy can watch
more than crypto

The interesting part is simple: some Binance-connected futures can track familiar real-world markets without buying the asset itself. Gold when fear rises, oil when inflation moves, broad ETFs when markets rotate, and AI-chip stocks when tech leads. Piano turns supported markets into rules you approve and monitor.

01 / Macro Signals

Gold, silver, and oil tell a macro story

Gold is watched when investors look for safety. Oil often reflects inflation, transport costs, and global demand. These markets give a strategy signals that are different from crypto momentum.

02 / Market Baskets

Trade the direction of a whole market

ETF-linked futures can express the flow of a broad market: U.S. large caps, Nasdaq tech, Korea, Japan, and other supported regions. One rule can watch a market basket instead of one coin.

03 / Big Themes

AI, chips, and crypto-linked equities

Selected stock-linked futures can reflect attention around big tech, AI semiconductors, electric vehicles, or crypto-linked companies. Availability changes by exchange, so Piano keeps execution inside approved instruments.

04 / Basis Hedge

Basis hedge as a core edge

Piano is not only about guessing direction. Basis hedge remains a core edge: monitoring spot-futures or related market spreads, correcting drift, and executing only within user-approved conditions.
